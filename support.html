Pause and settings
How do I finish a dive?

Swim to the Moon Gate at the end. Each dive gives up to 3 stars: one for finishing, one for every coin and one for taking no hits. Stars unlock scarf colours, and coins buy upgrades in the Coral Shop.

I keep running out of air

Swim through bubbles to refill it. Running out costs one heart. If a dive is too hard, try Settings → Gameplay → Difficulty assist (Relaxed gives more air).

Up and down feel the wrong way round

Turn on invert up/down in Settings → Gameplay.

The DASH button is too small or too big

Change it in Settings → Touch controls → DASH button size.

The game runs slowly or my phone gets warm

Set Settings → Gameplay → Graphics to Low.

What are boosters?

Boosters are one-dive helpers: Shell Start (80 coins), Extra Heart (120), Big Tank (90) and Coin Magnet (60). Buy them with coins in the Coral Shop's Boosters tab, or win them from some missions. The Get ready panel before each dive lets you take up to 2. Retry reuses them if you still have some; Next dive and Practice reef don't use them. Coins can't be bought with money.

Who is Fin?

Fin is a small glowing fish who joins Pip after dive 4. Fin swims beside Pip and finds 10% extra coins on every dive (shown as "Fin found +N" on the results screen). Fin never changes your stars.

How do I turn off the characters' speech bubbles?

Turn off Settings → Gameplay → Character chat. The guides never pause the game while they talk. You can read about every friend you have met in the Wardrobe.

Does it work offline?

Yes. The whole game works offline. Only cloud saves and ads need a connection.

Progress & cloud saves

How do I keep my progress on a new phone?

Sign in to Game Center on iPhone and iPad, or Google Play Games on Android. The game keeps a copy of your progress in that account and restores it on any device signed in to the same account. You can check the status in Settings → Saved progress.

Without it, progress stays on the device.

My progress didn't come back after reinstalling

Make sure you're signed in to the same Game Center or Google Play Games account as before, then open Settings → Saved progress and wait a moment while it syncs. If it still doesn't appear, email support.

How do I start again from the beginning?

Settings → Saved progress → Reset progress, then tap again to confirm. This can't be undone.

Ads & privacy

When do ads appear?

Only when you choose to watch one: to keep diving after you run out of hearts, or to double the coins from a dive. You can always start the dive over instead. The desktop version has no ads.

No ad is offered when I run out of hearts

You may be offline, or no ad is available right now. Try again later, or start the dive over.

How do I change my ad privacy choices?

In the game: Settings → About → Privacy choices (ads). On iPhone and iPad, tracking permission is in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

What data does the game collect?

Pip's Dive has no accounts and the developer collects no personal information. Google collects some data to show ads. Read the full privacy policy.

Problems & bugs

I found a bug

Email support with what happened, which dive you were on and, if you can, a screenshot or screen recording. It helps a lot.

There's no sound

Check the volumes in Settings → Sound, and that your device's volume is turned up. If it still doesn't play, email support.
