# Pip's Dive

A bright 3D underwater adventure about Pip, a little fox spirit, swimming through glowing tide
gates to the Moon Gate. 16 dives across 4 seas, a new Daily Dive every day, and a Coral Shop of
upgrades to buy with the coins you collect.

**▶ Play in your browser: https://nerojust.github.io/pips-dive-web/**

Works offline once loaded, and can be installed as an app from the browser menu. Keyboard,
mouse, touch and game controllers all work.
